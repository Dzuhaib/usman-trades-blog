# Usman Trades Content Writing Standards

## Purpose

This document defines the content generation standards for all articles published on Usman Trades.

The goal is to create content that is genuinely useful, educational, trustworthy, emotionally engaging, and written in a way that feels like it came from a real person with experience rather than an AI system.

Every article must prioritize helping the reader understand a topic completely.

The objective is not to generate content quickly.

The objective is to create the best answer available for the searcher's question.

---

# Core Constraints

*   **Length:** Every article must be between **1200 and 1800 words**. This ensures sufficient depth for complex financial topics while maintaining reader engagement.
*   **Formatting:** The use of **dashes (-)** in the article content is strictly prohibited. Use alternative punctuation or sentence structures to maintain clarity.

---

# Content Intent Requirements

Every article must target informational search intent.

The purpose of the content is to educate, explain, teach, guide, and help.

Content must never feel promotional.

Content must never push products, services, broker accounts, signals, or unrealistic outcomes.

The reader should leave the article with a deeper understanding of the topic than they had before arriving.

---

# Human Writing Requirements

Every article must feel written by a real human.

Content should contain natural language patterns.

Sentence lengths should vary naturally.

Paragraph lengths should vary naturally.

The writing should feel conversational while remaining professional.

Avoid robotic transitions.

Avoid repetitive sentence structures.

Avoid generic AI phrases.

Avoid sounding like a textbook.

Avoid sounding like marketing copy.

Avoid exaggerated authority.

Avoid pretending to be a financial institution.

Write like an experienced educator explaining concepts to a real person.

---

# Storytelling Requirements

Whenever appropriate, include realistic stories and examples.

Examples should feel believable and relatable.

Readers should be able to imagine themselves in the situation.

Stories should connect emotionally without becoming dramatic.

Good example:

A trader opens a position after risking far too much of their account because they feel confident after several winning trades. One unexpected market move wipes out weeks of gains. This experience illustrates why position sizing matters more than finding perfect entries.

Stories should support learning.

Stories should never be fictional fantasies.

Stories should feel grounded in real world situations.

---

# Originality Requirements

Every article must be unique.

Never rewrite existing content word for word.

Never duplicate sections across articles.

Never use repetitive templates that make multiple articles sound identical.

Every article should bring a fresh perspective while remaining factually accurate.

Content should demonstrate original thought and clear reasoning.

---

# SEO Requirements

Every article must be optimized for search engines while maintaining readability.

The primary keyword must appear naturally throughout the content.

Use semantic variations and related topics.

Include supporting keywords naturally.

Avoid keyword stuffing.

Write for humans first and search engines second.

Every article should target one primary keyword and multiple supporting keywords.

Use clear heading hierarchy.

Use descriptive headings.

Use meaningful internal links.

Use contextual references to related content.

Optimize title tags and meta descriptions.

Focus on search intent satisfaction above all else.

---

# AEO Requirements

Every article must be optimized for Answer Engine Optimization.

Content should answer questions directly.

Important questions should receive concise answers before deeper explanations.

Include question based headings.

Provide clear definitions.

Provide direct answers early in sections.

Create content that can be used in featured snippets.

Create content that can be used in AI generated search summaries.

Use simple language when explaining complex concepts.

Structure information logically.

Anticipate follow up questions.

Answer those questions proactively.

---

# Expertise Requirements

All financial information must be accurate.

All calculations must be mathematically correct.

All educational explanations must be factually reliable.

Never make guarantees.

Never promise profits.

Never claim certainty about future market movements.

Always present trading as a probabilistic activity.

Always emphasize risk management.

Always prioritize capital preservation over profit discussions.

---

# Article Structure

Every article must follow this exact structure.

## Introduction

Introduce the topic clearly.

Explain why the topic matters.

Address the reader's likely question.

Provide a preview of what the article will cover.

---

## Main Concept

Explain the core idea in simple language.

Break down difficult concepts.

Use examples where appropriate.

Build understanding progressively.

---

## Practical Example

Provide a realistic example.

Show how the concept works in practice.

Use numbers when helpful.

Use scenarios that traders commonly experience.

Make the example easy to follow.

---

## Common Mistakes

Explain the most common errors people make.

Show why those mistakes occur.

Explain how readers can avoid them.

Focus on practical learning.

---

## Risk Considerations

Explain potential risks.

Discuss limitations.

Address situations where the concept may not work as expected.

Promote responsible decision making.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Answer common questions directly.

Use concise answers.

Expand where necessary.

Focus on real user concerns.

---

## Related Tools

Recommend relevant calculators or tools available on the website.

Only recommend tools that genuinely help the reader.

Provide context for why the tool is useful.

---

## Related Articles

Link to relevant educational resources.

Recommend content that expands understanding.

Ensure recommendations are contextually relevant.

---

## Conclusion

Summarize the key takeaways.

Reinforce the most important lessons.

Leave the reader with practical next steps.

End naturally without sales language.

---

# Readability Standards

Use short paragraphs.

Use clear language.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Explain technical terms when introduced.

Prioritize clarity over complexity.

The best article is not the one that sounds smartest.

The best article is the one readers understand and remember.

---

# Trust Standards

Be transparent.

Be honest.

Acknowledge uncertainty where appropriate.

Present balanced information.

Never manipulate readers.

Never create fear.

Never create false urgency.

Build trust through usefulness.

---

# Final Rule

Before publishing any article, ask:

Does this genuinely help the reader understand the topic better?

If the answer is no, rewrite the content until the answer becomes yes.
