# Google AdSense Compliance Guide for Usman Trades

## Overview

This guide ensures all published content complies with Google AdSense policies to prevent ad disapproval, account suspension, or revenue loss.

---

## 1. Original and Substantive Content

### Policy Requirement
Google AdSense requires that all content be **original, substantive, and created for a genuine audience** — not generated purely to manipulate search rankings or serve ads.

### Rules
- Every article must provide real value to the reader
- Content must not be auto-generated or spun
- Articles must demonstrate **E-E-A-T** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- Minimum **1300 words** per article to ensure substantive depth
- Content must not duplicate other articles on the site or elsewhere

### How to Verify
- Ask: "Would a reader genuinely learn something from this?"
- If the answer is no, rewrite until the answer is yes
- Every article must have a clear author with real credentials

---

## 2. No Misleading or Deceptive Content

### Policy Requirement
Content must not contain misleading claims, false promises, or deceptive practices.

### Rules
- **Never** promise guaranteed profits or specific returns
- **Never** claim certainty about future market movements
- **Never** use fake countdown timers or urgency tactics
- **Never** claim "risk-free" trading
- **Never** impersonate financial institutions or regulatory bodies
- Trading must be presented as a **probabilistic activity** with inherent risk

### Required Disclaimers
- All trading involves significant risk of loss
- Past performance does not guarantee future results
- Content is for educational purposes only — not financial advice
- This must be visible on every page (Footer and Disclaimer page)

---

## 3. Proper Ad Placement and Limits

### Policy Requirement
Ads must not disrupt or interfere with the content. Ad placement must follow Google's policies.

### Rules
- Maximum **3 AdSense ad units** per page
- Maximum **2 link units** per page
- Maximum **2 search boxes** per page
- Ads must not be placed **above the fold** in a way that pushes content down
- Ads must not be placed in a way that confuses users into clicking them
- Ensure proper spacing between ads and content to avoid accidental clicks

### AdSense-Friendly Layout
- Place ads within the article (after paragraphs)
- Use **inline content ads** that match the surrounding text
- Place one ad unit near the top, one in the middle, one near the end
- Use native-looking display ads that blend with the content

---

## 4. No Prohibited Content

### Policy Requirement
Certain topics are strictly prohibited for AdSense monetization.

### Never Include
- Content promoting illegal activities
- Content depicting violence or gratuitous harm
- Copyright infringement or stolen content
- Hate speech or discriminatory content
- Adult or sexually explicit material
- Content about hacking or piracy
- Content promoting tobacco, drugs, or alcohol
- **Financial trading signals or recommendations** (use educational content only)
- Promises of guaranteed returns or unrealistic outcomes

### Our Approach
- All content is **educational only** — teaching risk management and market mechanics
- No trade signals are ever given
- No specific broker or platform is endorsed
- All examples are hypothetical and for learning purposes

---

## 5. E-E-A-T Compliance

### Policy Requirement
Google's Quality Rater Guidelines emphasize **Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness**.

### Requirements

**Experience:**
- Write from personal experience or observation
- Include real-world examples and case studies
- Use first-hand knowledge of trading and risk management

**Expertise:**
- Display clear author credentials (e.g., "Senior Market Analyst")
- Demonstrate deep knowledge of the topic
- Use accurate, mathematically correct information

**Authoritativeness:**
- Build trust through consistent, high-quality content
- Link to reputable sources
- Maintain an editorial policy and disclaimer page

**Trustworthiness:**
- Be transparent about limitations and uncertainties
- Never manipulate readers or create false urgency
- Acknowledge when information may not be certain
- Provide balanced perspectives

---

## 6. Real-World Examples Requirement

### Policy Requirement
Google rewards content that provides practical, actionable information.

### Requirements
- Every article must include **at least 2-3 real-world examples**
- Examples must use realistic numbers and scenarios
- Include specific dollar amounts, percentages, and outcomes
- Show both successful and unsuccessful scenarios
- Examples should be relatable to actual traders

### Example Format
> "A trader with a $10,000 account risks 1% ($100) per trade with a 20-pip stop loss on EUR/USD. Using the position sizing formula, the recommended lot size is 0.50 lots. If the stop loss is hit, the trader loses exactly $100 — no more, no less."

---

## 7. Technical Compliance

### Page Quality Requirements
- Fast loading times (optimize images, minimize JavaScript)
- Mobile-friendly design
- Secure HTTPS connection
- Clean navigation and clear page structure
- No pop-ups or interstitials that block content
- Proper grammar and spelling throughout

### Content Structure
- Clear headings and subheadings (H1, H2, H3 hierarchy)
- Short paragraphs for readability
- Bold key terms and numbers for emphasis
- Include FAQ sections for featured snippets
- Use internal links to related content
- Include image alt text for accessibility

---

## 8. AdSense Application Checklist

Before applying for or maintaining AdSense, verify:

- [ ] All articles are 1300+ words
- [ ] No article contains promises of profit
- [ ] All trading content has clear risk disclaimers
- [ ] No copyrighted or stolen content
- [ ] Author credentials are displayed prominently
- [ ] Editorial policy and disclaimer pages exist
- [ ] No more than 3 AdSense ad units per page
- [ ] Ads do not interfere with content
- [ ] Content is original and not duplicated
- [ ] Site has a clear "About Us" or author section
- [ ] All links are properly labeled and functional
- [ ] No malware, phishing, or security issues

---

## 9. Common Violations to Avoid

1. **Keyword stuffing** — Do not repeat keywords unnaturally
2. **Cloaking** — Do not show different content to users vs. search engines
3. **Hidden text** — Do not hide text by matching it to the background
4. **Doorway pages** — Do not create pages designed only for search engines
5. **Auto-generated content** — Do not use AI to produce thin, meaningless content
6. **Misleading ads** — Do not place ads that look like navigation or buttons
7. **Incorrect AdSense code** — Do not modify the AdSense code in any way
8. **Click encouragement** — Do not use language like "click here" to encourage ad clicks

---

## 10. Review Process

Every article must be reviewed before publishing:

1. **Word count check** — Minimum 1300 words
2. **Real-world example check** — At least 2-3 examples included
3. **AdSense compliance check** — No promises, disclaimers present
4. **Grammar and spelling check** — Clean, professional writing
5. **E-E-A-T check** — Author credited, expertise demonstrated
6. **Internal links check** — Links to related tools and articles
7. **Image check** — Proper alt text, images relevant
8. **FAQ section check** — At least 3-5 FAQ items included
